Network connectivity issues can also affect Yandex Disk account login. If the login page does not load or the process times out, check your internet connection and try again. Firewall or proxy settings may also interfere with the Yandex Disk account login. Disabling VPN services temporarily can help determine if they are causing the issue.

If you forget to log out on someone else's device, you can go to Yandex ID, navigate to the Security section, and click "Log out on all devices." This terminates all active sessions and secures your account.
